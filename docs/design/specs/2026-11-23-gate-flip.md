# The unzoom gate that flips every frame

Written 2026-11-23, after soak 21 on 0.6.0-feel.46. This build (branch `round8`) changes one thing the player can
see (the kept speed, part 4) and otherwise only logs. The fix for the cause waits for what the two new log lines
show in the game.

## The finding

Standing still with the LAS-5 Scythe in the native view, the first-person rig's eye went about 0.2 m back and forth
along the view's forward axis **every frame**, for 2.3 seconds (soak 21, 03:13:50 to 03:13:53). The player saw the
world shake at 15 Hz and, at the next event, the view thrown 0.435 m past the far place.

The three reports, all under `~/.cache/tfp-altdiag` on the machine that ran the soak:

- `REPORT-B.md`: the cause. The game's own evaluator of camera kind 6 turns the lens towards the wide third-person
  target (90 degrees) while an "unzoom gate" is set, and towards the weapon's own (60 degrees for the Scythe) while
  it is clear. The gate was flipping every frame. The proof is the lens number: a target that flips 60/90 each frame
  settles the lens at exactly 67.6 degrees after a gated frame and 63.8 after a clear one, whatever the frame time,
  and the log shows those two numbers for the whole episode. The rig eye follows the gate.
- `REPORT-A.md`: what the player saw, and why the eye's continuity let it through: it looks for jumps only for
  600 ms after an event, and the flipping went on long after. A guard that holds the eye in the middle was tried
  there (`proto/camera_continuity.h`).
- `REPORT-C.md`: a sceptic's review. The same signature is in 19 of 21 soak logs, back to 0.6.0-feel.37; in soak 19
  it ran for three seconds after a sprint stopped, with no weapon swap at all. It is not a rare race at one entry.

What is **known**: the lens target flips each frame through the game's gated branch; the rig eye moves with it; the
presented camera is not part of the loop (the eye kept flipping while the continuity held the presented eye still).

What is **not known**:

- Which of the two gate inputs flips: the avatar's packed bit 0 (copied from byte +5 of the avatar's main row), or
  the request's byte +0x8f. The reports lean to the avatar bit (65 to 75 in 100).
- Whether the mod is part of the loop. The mod's zoom-depth term (`src/pose_helper.c`, the reason-4 return in
  `depth_delta`) is dropped to nothing on every gated frame and added on every clear one, and its pair shifts the
  rig root along the same forward axis. If its size is near 0.2 m, the mod makes the shake itself and may also be
  what keeps the gate flipping. Until this build nothing of it reached the log.

To find episodes in any mod log:

```
grep -nE "fov_native=1\.(113[5-9]|114[0-2]|180[3-8])|asked for (63\.[89]|67\.6) deg" <modlog>
```

Known episodes: `~/.cache/tfp-soak-19.modlog` 07:03:55 to 07:03:58, `~/.cache/tfp-soak-21.modlog` 03:13:50 to
03:13:53.

## What this build logs

### `Gate:` (src/gate_log.h, counted in src/pose_helper.c, written by src/mod_view.c)

One line in a second in which a gate byte changed or the zoom depth was added in some frames and passed in others,
and once in the first second of each native entry (it then ends "the first second of a native entry"). A calm
second writes nothing. A start line says it is watched: `Ready: unzoom gate watched: ...`.

```
Gate: 30 frames of the rig root, the lens read in 30; avatar bit set in 15, changed 30 times; request+0x8f set in 0, changed 0 times; zoom depth added in 15 (-0.2010 to -0.1995 m), nothing to add in 0, passed in 15 (unzoom gate or request state 15); lens target 60.0 to 90.0 degrees, lens 63.8 to 67.6; request+0x88 = 1.000, +0x8d = 0; main row read in 30: +5 set in 15, changed 30 times, +0x3d set in 30, +0x11ac 0.4375 to 0.5210
```

(That is how a second of the soak 21 episode would read **if** the avatar bit is the one that flips and the mod's
depth is about 0.2 m. The frame count, the lens target and the lens are from that log; the rest is made up.)

How to read it:

- **frames of the rig root**: frames in which the helper wrote the rig's root node, one a game frame while a lease
  runs. **the lens read in**: of those, the frames in which the evaluator's lens locals were read; the gate bytes are
  only counted there.
- **avatar bit set in N, changed M times** and **request+0x8f set in N, changed M times**: the two gate inputs. In
  an episode one of them reads about half the frames set and about as many changes as frames. That one is the gate
  that flips. The other should read 0 changes.
- **zoom depth added in N (least to most m)**: frames in which the mod's depth term shifted the rig (and its root
  the other way), and by how much along the rig's forward axis. **nothing to add**: the term was worked out and came
  to nothing (the widest optic). **passed in N (reason count, ...)**: frames it was not applied, by reason. "unzoom
  gate or request state" is the reason-4 return, the one that follows the gate. Other reasons: full aim, no lens read
  for the frame, values out of range, weapon data not read, previous distance not known, over 1 m, weapon data
  changed, zoom policy, depth_off.txt, no evaluator frame, the evaluator's code is not this build's.
- **lens target a to b degrees, lens c to d**: 60.0 to 90.0 and 63.8 to 67.6 is the fingerprint.
- **request+0x88, +0x8d**: as last read in the second.
- A value that is no number is left out of the least and most; "the lens read was no number" and "+0x11ac no number"
  say that none of the second's values was one.
- **main row read in N: +5 set in .., changed .. times, +0x3d set in .., +0x11ac a to b**: the avatar's main row
  (avatar owner + 0x53d8b0 + index * 0x1238) as it stood when the mod wrote the rig root. Byte +5 is what the game
  copies into the avatar bit; by the review's reading of the code it is `+0x3d && (flag == 0 || scalar > 0)` and the
  scalar is the float stored at +0x11ac. If +5 flips while +0x3d stays set, the float is the margin to look at.
  "main row not readable": the two guarded reads failed. The vec3 at +0x10e4 the review also asked for is left out.
- Ends with **depth_off.txt: the mod's zoom depth is forced to nothing** when the switch is on.

### `depth_off.txt` (the depth switch)

A file of that name beside the DLL, read once at the start, makes the zoom-depth term add nothing on every frame
(reason 11, "depth_off.txt" in the Gate: line). Said at the start:

```
Test: depth_off.txt: the mod's zoom depth is forced to nothing on every frame (neither the rig nor its root is shifted for the optic)
```

With it on, the weapon sits where the game puts it for a zoomed optic, without the mod's correction. That is the
price of the test and is expected.

### `Alternation:` (src/camera_alternation.h, counted in src/camera_helper.c)

The detection rule of the guard that was tried, **acting on nothing**: it only counts. A move is a frame whose
wanted eye is 0.05 m or more from where it was; it is a return when it ends within a quarter of its own length of
the place left by the move before, no more than 70 ms earlier; frames count from the second return in a row until a
move that is no return or 70 ms without a move; never in full aim. One line in a second that has such frames:

```
Alternation: 30 frames of the eye going back and forth (would be held; nothing is done), largest move 0.231 m; jumps were looked for in 0 of them; the fire flag was set in 0 (flag byte 12 bit 0x40, not proven on this build); kind 6
```

(About what the seconds ending 03:13:52.020 and 03:13:53.021 of soak 21 would read. The second before would show
some 5 frames, all of them looked at; the second after some 16, three of them looked at.)

How to read it:

- A line beside a fingerprint hit is the guard seeing the episode. Lines **without** a fingerprint hit nearby are
  what the guard would have flattened for another reason: recoil, an explosion's shake, a stagger. Those are its
  false positives, and their number over a soak says whether a guard keyed on the motion alone is safe.
- **jumps were looked for in N**: in those frames the continuity was already taking the flips up itself.
- **the fire flag**: the controller's flag byte 12 bit 0x40, which the helper already reads. No reader of it is
  proven on this build, so a 0 does not prove the weapon was not firing. No read was added for it.

## What this build changes: the kept speed (src/camera_continuity.h, THE WAY BACK)

Where jumps are not looked for, a frame over the measure set the eye's kept speed to its own step whole. A flip of
0.2 m a frame was kept as 6 to 7 m/s, and the first frame looked at afterwards sent the presented eye on at it:
0.217 m and 0.179 m past the far place at 03:13:53.490, 0.435 m carried. Made up, an event 1.5 s into a flipping not
looked at ended 1.0 to 1.5 m from the middle.

Now a frame not looked at that takes the eye back to within a quarter of the move's length of where it was two
frames ago (the move being over the measure) keeps no speed. On the logged frames the presented eye steps 0.050 m at
most where it stepped 0.217 m, and stays 0.106 m from the middle (half the flip) where it was 0.50 m; made up, 0.105 m
at 8, 16.7, 33.3 and 50 ms a frame. A sprint, a dive and a single step keep their speed as before.

This does **not** stop the shake while nothing is looked for: the eye still follows the rig there. It only removes the
throw at the next event.

## What it costs

Nothing new runs in the tick but two takes once a second (a lock, a copy of some 200 bytes, and the line when one is
written), so the `Cost:` line's tick should read as before. In the game's own calls, per frame: the count at the rig
root write and its two guarded reads of the main row (the `Reads:` line gives 0.09 microseconds a read), the
alternation rule at the camera write (two distances), and two vector lengths more in the continuity.

## Experiments to run in the game

Each one: do it, then grep the mod log for the fingerprint and read the `Gate:` and `Alternation:` lines beside the
hits.

1. **An ordinary soak.** No special steps: the review found it several times a session. This alone names the gate.
2. **The provocation.** Scythe out, standing, the view 10 to 30 degrees down. Then either
   - sprint and stop dead, and stand for five seconds; or
   - press 3, turn the view about 60 degrees, press 1, and stand for five seconds.
   Repeat a few times at different places.
3. **The same with `depth_off.txt`** beside the DLL (restart the game with the file there; the log says
   `Test: depth_off.txt` at the start). Same provocation, same number of tries.
4. By eye only, as a cross-check: the same standing still with first person switched off but aiming.

## What each outcome means, and the fix order

**From the Gate: line in an episode (experiments 1 and 2):**

- *avatar bit set in about half the frames, changed about every frame*: the avatar bit is the gate. Read the main
  row: if +5 flips with it and +0x3d stays set, the game's spatial test at +0x11ac sits on its margin.
- *request+0x8f the same*: the reload-ability byte is the gate; the main row does not matter.
- *both steady while the lens still reads 63.8 to 67.6*: the reports' account is wrong; stop and look again.
- *zoom depth added in about half the frames, passed (unzoom gate or request state) in the other half, with a size
  near 0.2 m*: the mod makes the eye's shake itself, whatever flips the gate. Fix A below is due on its own account.
- *nothing to add, or a size of a few millimetres*: the 0.2 m is the game's own depth blend; fix A will not help the
  eye.

**From the same with depth_off.txt (experiment 3):**

- *no fingerprint hits any more*: the mod's root shift closes the loop. Fix A should end the flipping itself.
- *hits remain, with standing `eye_jump` above 0.3 in the View: line*: the loop is the game's own, and what is left
  is the game's share of the shake. It can then only be hidden (fix C).
- *hits remain but the eye's steps are small*: the gate flips by itself but the shake was the mod's: fix A is enough
  for the eye; the lens still breathes 63.8/67.6, which the field-of-view lock already hides.

**The order:**

1. This build: the two lines, the switch, the kept speed.
2. **Fix A**, if the depth is non-zero and follows the gate: at the reason-4 return in `depth_delta`
   (`src/pose_helper.c`), do not drop the term on a gated frame; scale the last supported correction by where the
   lens is between the zoomed and the wide target. The lens cannot jump (2 rad/s at most), so the mod's share of a
   flip falls to about an eighth, and a reload or a dive is eased instead of stepped. The root's pair follows by
   itself. Then soak again with the grep.
3. **Fix C**, if the fingerprint persists with the depth steady or off: a guard keyed on the gate, not on the
   motion: act only while the logged gate byte has changed within the last two frames, hold the eye to the gate-clear
   place, and hold the rig and root pair to the same so the gun does not take the shake over. It cannot flatten recoil.
4. The guard keyed on the motion alone (what `Alternation:` counts): only if its lines over a soak show no false
   positives, and not as the only measure.

## What is not covered

- The Gate: line's counting and the depth switch are tested against the pose helper on the test world
  (`tests/run-wire-pose.sh`) and by themselves (`tests/run-gate-log.sh`); the main row's offsets are from the research
  notes and have not been read in the game yet. If the line says "main row not readable", or +5 never matches the
  avatar bit, the offsets are wrong for this build and the row's numbers must not be used.
- The main row is read when the mod writes the rig root, the gate bytes when the evaluator reads the rig eye, a
  little earlier in the same frame. Whether the game writes the row before or after that point in a frame is not
  known, so +5 can read one frame ahead of or behind the avatar bit.
- A single back-and-forth not looked at, followed at once by an event, still meets the filter with the first flip's
  speed kept: the first move of a flipping is not a return yet.

## Round 9: the rig's depth held steady

Written 2026-11-24, after soak 22 on 0.6.0-feel.47 (20 minutes; `~/.cache/tfp-soak-22.modlog`). Branch `round9`.
The version string is not changed.

### What feel.47 showed in the game

- **The avatar bit is the gate that flips.** In every episode the avatar's packed bit 0 and the main row's byte +5
  change in the same counts; request+0x8f never changes with them (8 changes in the whole soak, none in a flipping
  second). Example, 05:20:52.953: 27 frames, the bit set in 17, changed 16 times, lens target 60.0 to 90.0.
- **The mod's zoom depth term is not in it.** It added something in 0 frames of the session: "weapon data not read" on
  every clear frame, "unzoom gate" on every gated one. So `depth_off.txt` changes nothing and Fix A has nothing to fix
  on this build.
- **Two shapes of episode.** Every frame (05:20:52, and bursts of 7 or 8 changes), and a slow cycle of about eight
  frames set and two clear for three seconds (05:23:53, 05:24:00, 05:25:58), the lens between 77 and 89 degrees.
  The `Alternation:` rule saw 6 frames in 20 minutes: a guard keyed on the motion pattern would have missed nearly
  all of it.
- **The lens lock holds.** In every flipping second the `Fov:` line reads "+60 frames locked; the game asked for
  62.5 to 90.0 degrees, shown 80.0". The lens is not what the player sees move.
- The wanted eye in the five traced frames before 05:20:52.853 goes 0.141, 0.149, 0.188, 0.155, 0.187 m back and
  forth along the pitched view axis: steps of changing size, not one fixed distance.

### The cause, as far as it is established

Read in the game image (`native-work/evidence/game-pre1020.dll`, file offset = game RVA); none of it traced in the
running game.

1. **Who sets the bit.** One producer, in the avatar update (game+0x829e90): main row +5 is set when +0x3d is set and
   either the aim ray's first hit is within about 1 m of the ray's origin or a short probe (1 m, or a per-weapon
   length) hits anything that is not the player's own. In camera kind 6 the origin of both is the camera's own place
   in the world (context+0x3c), read back from the camera unit after the position call the mod's camera hook sits on.
   It is the game's "something is right in front of the camera" test. In the base game kind 6 is only the full aim;
   the mod keeps it open at the hip, where the probe finds things often.
2. **What the bit does.** In the kind-6 evaluator a set bit switches the lens target to the wide one (game+0x1286ad9).
3. **Where the depth comes from** (game+0x12873b3..0x1287423):

   ```
   span = |target - request+0xa0|
   D = request+0x90                              where span is nothing
   D = (1-t)*request+0x90 + t*request+0x9c       else, t = min(1, |target - lens| / span)
   ```

   `target` is the value the gate has just switched; `lens` cannot jump. +0x90 is the selected optic's distance, +0x9c
   the one before (0 on a fresh request), +0xa0 the lens when they were set. D is added to the forward value of the
   rig's vector (game+0x1287a91), which goes to node 5 (request+0x4c) and, negated and turned by the final rotation,
   to the rig's root (game+0x1287c24). The eye hangs on the root, the camera on the eye.
4. **So:** with the lens resting on its target, t is 0 and D is +0x90, gate set or clear: the two settled places are
   the same place. A change of the gate throws the target to the other end, t jumps, D jumps towards the distance
   before, and the eye goes along the view in one frame and creeps home as the lens travels. A gate that changes
   every frame does that every frame. The steps of changing size in the trace fit a t that depends on where the lens
   is; a fixed offset would not.
5. **The loop.** The eye's place is the origin of the test that sets the bit, one frame later. Where a set bit takes
   the camera away from what the probe touched, the bit inverts every frame.

The two diagnoses of round 9 disagreed on one point. One had the game scale the rig's whole vector by main row
+0x11ac on every gated frame (game+0x1287b76). The listing has a branch before it (0x1287ab1): that scaling only runs
while request+0x8d is set and the weight at +0x88 is under 1, the 75 ms after a reload state changed. The log reads
+0x88 = 1.000 in every episode. The depth blend is the standing mechanism; the scaling is a second, short one.

**Not proven:** the size. request+0x90, +0x9c and +0xa0 never reached the log, so that the blend gives the 0.14 to
0.2 m seen is an inference (it needs `(t_set - t_clear) * (d9c - d90)` of that size, for instance a request built at
the mod's locked 80 degrees with a distance of about 0.2 m). Also not proven: that the camera's place closes the loop
(about 70 in 100), and what the probe hits.

### What the fix does (src/depth_steady.h, src/pose_helper.c)

In the hip view the mod locks the shown field of view, so the lens the game's depth follows is not shown. The depth
has no reason to follow it. On every frame of the rig's node 5 that has the evaluator's lens locals (the ones the
zoom depth term already reads, with the same proofs that they belong to this call), the helper now:

- works out the game's own D for the frame by the formula above, mirrored to the float;
- keeps a **held distance**: it begins at the game's own D when a lease begins (so nothing is added in the first
  frame and there is no step into it) and goes to the settled distance (request+0x90; or the mod's own reference for
  the optic where the zoom depth term has one for exactly these distances) in a critically damped ease of its own,
  nine tenths of the way in 216 ms, at any frame time; again whenever the settled distance changes (another optic);
- adds `share * gain * (held - D)` to node 5's forward value and takes the same from the root along the view's
  forward: the existing zoom depth pair, through its own guarded writes (same request, same evaluation, the pinned
  code). The rig, the gun, the arms and the eye stay together; the eye is the camera's wanted place, so the camera
  hook and the continuity filter get a still eye and have nothing to take up.

`gain` is how much of D the game put into the vector this frame (1; the reload weight or its rest during the 75 ms
blend; 0 while request+0x8f is set and settled). `share` is the hip share: 1 at the hip, 0 in full aim, eased by the
placement's own blend, so in full aim the game's own depth is shown whole (the zoom-in is the game's) and the
hand-over has no step. For that the root's half is now also written on the way into and out of full aim, where
retail's depth pair is not.

While the lens rests on its target the term is nothing, to the bit: a calm frame goes out exactly as before.

It is **not keyed on the gate bit and not on a motion pattern**. It takes the gate's lever (the lens target) out of
the depth on every frame. That is why Fix C's rule "while the bit changed within the last two frames" is not used:
once the eye is held the bit stops changing, the guard would let go two frames later, the step would go through and
the loop start again, slower. A single honest change of the gate (aiming, a swap, walking up to a wall) used to give
one jump and a creep back to the same place; now it gives nothing, because both ends are the same settled distance.

Where it has a number, it goes out **in place of** the zoom depth term's (which is dropped on every gated frame). On
this build that term adds nothing anyway. If its weapon data read is ever repaired, the two agree at rest (the held
distance then rests on the term's reference) and the term's own blend over the lens is left out.

Off at the start in the helper (the differential test against retail is unchanged); the binder switches it on.

### Three guards (after the review of the branch, 2026-11-24)

- **The code pin.** The lens locals are the evaluator's stack, read by offset, and the formula mirrors the
  evaluator's code. The zoom depth term refuses unless its nine code pins are this build's; the hold used the same
  locals without asking. It asks now (once a lease, the term's own check), and where a pin is not this build's every
  frame is passed, counted as "the evaluator's code is not the pinned one". Nothing is added on node 5 or the root.
- **No shift over 1 m.** Each value can be inside its own range (distances up to 4 m either way) and the rule still
  ask for 8 m. A shift of more than 1 m (the zoom depth term's own limit) is passed as "values out of range" and the
  held distance is left as it was.
- **Node 5 never keeps moving without its root.** The root's half is a second call of the game's, with proofs of its
  own, and it is live on this build for the first time: on main the zoom depth term adds nothing, so the pair was
  never written. If that call is refused or does not come, node 5 moves alone and the gun goes against the eye,
  which is worse than main. So the pair is counted: a frame in which node 5's float was changed owes a root; after
  **two** such frames running without one, or **three** in the lease whether running or not, the held depth stops
  for that lease (passed as "the root did not follow", the game's depth still worked out and counted, as with the
  switch), and one line says so, once, with the lease's count and whether the last of them were running:

  ```
  Steady: stopped for lease 7: the rig was moved in 2 frames of this lease and its root did not follow (the last 2 running), so the gun moved against the eye in them; nothing more is held in this lease, and it begins again at the next
  Steady: stopped for lease 8: the rig was moved in 3 frames of this lease and its root did not follow (no two of them running), so the gun moved against the eye in them; nothing more is held in this lease, and it begins again at the next
  ```

  It begins again only at a new lease. Two running, not one: a single frame can lose its root to a request that
  changed between the two calls, and that is borne. Two, not three: each frame alone is a frame of the fault this
  guards against. A frame in which the root followed begins the count of frames running again; a frame in which
  nothing was moved changes nothing. After a stop the lease is as on main: the zoom depth term's own number, where
  it has one.

  **The count for the lease (after the second check, 2026-11-24).** Frames running alone are not enough: a root that
  comes every other frame begins that count again each time, and node 5 then moved alone in 60 of 120 frames with no
  stop (40 of 120 with the root in two frames of three, 12 of 120 in nine of ten). So the frames alone are also
  counted over the lease, and a root that follows does not take any of them back. **In one lease node 5 is moved
  alone in at most 3 frames, whatever the pattern** (`TFP_STEADY_ALONE_LEASE`): a frame alone is counted at the
  beginning of the next frame, before anything is moved in it, and the frame that reaches either number moves
  nothing, nor does any after it. A lease in which the root follows in every frame it is asked for counts nothing,
  however long it runs; one lost frame is borne, and a second apart from it. The price: a long lease that loses a
  root three times, minutes apart, to a request that changed between the two calls also stops, and is as on main
  until the next lease. The stop line says so; if it shows up in a soak with "no two of them running", the number
  is what to look at, not the rule.

"Moved" means node 5's float changed. A shift worked out can be too small for the float (the lens one step short of
its target gives under 1e-7 m); such a frame moves nothing, asks nothing of the root and is counted in neither
figure. Before, it was counted as moved with no root, which read as a refusal and would have tripped the guard
above. "Its root with it" counts the frames in which the root's half was done, to the float.

**Left out: beginning again at the settled distance.** When the held distance begins again inside an episode on a
gated frame (a pause over 250 ms, a frame without the lens, a new lease), it begins at the game's gated depth and
eases home in 216 ms; beginning at the settled distance would save that ease. It was asked for only if it makes no
step in the first frame of an ordinary lease, and that cannot be shown. The first frame of a new native entry with
the gate set has the same numbers as a restart inside an episode: in the made-up scene "right after a swap" the
game's depth in that frame is 0.02 to 0.14 m (by the frame time) and the settled one 0.24 m, inside the gate's travel
by any measure the frame offers. The helper would add 0.10 to 0.22 m in the first frame of that lease, and nothing
it reads says what was on the screen the frame before. Beginning at the game's own depth adds nothing in the first frame, in every case. It stays.

### What it cannot do

**An improvement is expected, not a cure.** In soak 22 there are 31 seconds in which the avatar bit changed twice or
more. In 10 of them the lens target did not change at all (05:28:40.978: the bit set in 6 of 22 frames, changed 8
times, lens target 90.0 to 90.0, lens 90.0 to 90.0). With the target on the lens t is 0 on every frame, the game's
depth cannot move with the bit, and the hold adds nothing there. Whatever the player saw in those seconds is not the
depth blend. Still open for them: whether the eye moved at all in those seconds (the `View:` line's `eye_step`
beside them), and if it did, what else in the rig's vector follows the bit ("the rig's depth argument" gated against
clear, and main row +0x11ac, which reads 0.0000 to 0.2013 in that same second).

- **The bit may still change** on a true margin, and its other readers (a presentation byte on the body, a HUD path)
  still follow it. Nothing the mod writes holds the bit, on purpose.
- **Up against a wall** the game means to move the view while the lens travels; the held depth stays at the settled
  distance. Both ends are the same place, so only the travel is lost.
- **The 75 ms after a reload state change with the gate set**: the game scales the rig's whole vector by main row
  +0x11ac. Passed and counted ("the game scales the rig by main row +0x11ac"). If episodes remain and that count is
  not 0 in them, that is the next thing to undo.
- **With request+0x8d clear** the depth also enters the game's four-pass solve of the rotation; the hold moves the
  position only. The log read +0x8d = 1 throughout.
- **A frame without the evaluator's lens** is passed: nothing is added, on node 5 or the root.
- If the 0.2 m does not come from the depth blend at all, the hold adds nothing useful, and the line below says so.

### `Steady:` (src/depth_steady.h, counted in src/pose_helper.c, written by src/mod_view.c)

One line in a second in which the rig was moved, a frame was passed for its values or for the game's scaling, more
than two frames had no lens or no pinned code, or the held depth stopped for a lease; and after a stop, as with the
switch, in a second in which the game's own depth moved by more than a millimetre. A calm second writes nothing.

```
Steady: 30 frames of the rig, the game's depth worked out in 30: 0.0000 to 0.0000 m with the gate set (15 frames), 0.2400 to 0.2400 m clear (15); held at 0.2400 to 0.2400 m; the rig moved to it in 15 frames, its root with it in 15, by 0.2400 to 0.2400 m; the rig's depth argument 0.0700 to 0.0700 m gated, 0.3100 to 0.3100 m clear; passed in 0
```

(Made up, from the test.)

- **the game's depth ... with the gate set (N frames), ... clear (N)**: the game's own D, least and most, apart for
  gated and clear frames. **This is the proof or the refutation of the cause.** In an episode the two ranges should
  differ by about what the eye used to move (0.14 to 0.2 m). If both read the same single value while the `Gate:`
  line shows the bit flipping, the depth blend is not where the shake comes from.
- **held at a to b m**: the held distance. One value in a calm lease; a range while it eases (a lease beginning,
  another optic).
- **the rig moved to it in N frames, its root with it in M, by a to b m**: frames in which node 5's float was
  changed and by how much (plus: further back along the view), and of those the frames in which the root's half was
  done. M should equal N. Every frame fewer is a frame that lost its root (the gun moved against the eye in it).
  M under N does not by itself come with a stop: one lost frame is borne, and a second apart from it, with no line
  of their own, and the two counts are of one second while the stop counts over the lease. Two running, or the
  third of a lease, stop the held depth for the lease, and the `Steady: stopped for lease ...` line stands in the
  second it happened. So a lease shows N - M of at most 3 summed over all its seconds; more than that is a fault in
  the count, not a pattern it allows.
- **the rig's depth argument ... gated, ... clear**: the game's whole forward value for node 5, before the mod adds
  anything. If this differs between gated and clear frames by more than the depth does, something else in the vector
  follows the gate.
- **passed in N (reason count, ...)**: no lens read for the frame; values out of range (a value, or a shift over
  1 m); the game scales the rig by main row +0x11ac; depth_steady_off.txt; the evaluator's code is not the pinned
  one; the root did not follow (every frame of a lease after its stop).
- The `Gate:` line beside it: "passed in N (..., the depth held steady instead M)" are frames in which the zoom depth
  term had a number of its own and the held one went out.

### What the listing proves (game image, file offset = RVA; not traced in the running game)

1. **The zoom levels.** The kind-6 evaluator calls game+0x759b10 for the weapon's magnification: it picks one of three
   floats of the optic's data, +0x74, +0x78 or +0x7c, by an index from game+0x1787500 (0, 1, 2: the three scope
   ranges). The lens target is the third-person field of view (game+0x3326340 -> +0xac408, the value the helper's
   `source` reads) divided by that magnification, in degrees, then turned to radians (game+0x1286a76..0x1286a90).
   While the gate is set (the avatar's packed bit 0, or request+0x8f) the target is the third-person field of view
   itself and the magnification register is 1 (game+0x1286ae9..0x1286afa).
2. **The lens step** (game+0x1286ca8..0x1286d09, game+0x173cb30): with request+0x8d set (the log reads +0x8d = 1
   throughout) the lens moves toward the target by `clamp(10 |target - lens|, 0.1, 2) * m * dt` and never past it,
   `m` the magnification while the gate is clear and 1 while it is set. With +0x8d clear it is the target at once.
3. **So a flipping gate in full aim** pushes the lens wide at 2 rad/s on every set frame (the target is 1 rad or more
   away), and a clear frame pulls it back by 10 m dt of what is left. The lens sits about 0.2/m rad off the optic's
   target and moves about 2 dt rad every frame: **the same angle at every zoom level**, so its share of the field of
   view shown, which is what the eye reads as zoom, grows with the magnification. That is the report's "especially at
   50 or 100 m".
4. **The depth** (round 9's formula) moves the eye along the view by up to `|+0x9c - +0x90|` on each change of the
   gate, in full aim as at the hip. Round 9's hold took it out at the hip only: its share followed the hip weight and
   is 0 in full aim by design.
5. **The lock.** With 'zoom when aiming' the lock's glide follows the hooked call's value in full aim (`lock_field`):
   whatever the lens does reaches the screen through an 80 ms low pass. With the option off the lock shows its own
   fixed value in full aim and the lens is not seen, which fits a shake that comes with the option.

### What only the game can show

- Whether the gate flips in full aim at all, with which weapons and where. The `Aim:` line says it: "the unzoom gate
  set in N, changed M times" in a second of full aim.
- The magnifications of the scope ranges, and that a range change shows as a change of "x... of the third-person".
- That the hooked call's value follows the lens as the model has it (the ratio rule assumes the value is the lens
  scaled in the tangent). If the `Aim:` line shows "shown" moving while "the optic's own lens target" is one value
  and the gate flips, the composition is not that.
- That holding the depth in full aim looks right with a scope: the gun no longer comes to the eye with the game's
  zoom-in travel; it eases there in about 0.2 s (inferred to be about as long; not seen).
- Not covered by a test: the root's half of the hold in full aim on the test world (it has no aim); the order of the
  note and the hooked call in the game (the listing puts the evaluator's dispatch before the setter's call,
  `B_GAME_FIRD_DISPATCH`; a note that comes after is used one frame late or, if older than the last hooked call, not
  at all).

### What to try in the game

1. A weapon with a scope range setting (any rifle whose range can be set to 25 / 50 / 100 m), 'zoom when aiming' on,
   the lock on. Aim for five seconds at each range, standing in the open; then the same close to a ledge or a rock
   (a metre or less in front of the barrel), in tall grass, and past a teammate's shoulder.
2. Change the range while aiming, at each step.
3. Grep: `grep -nE "Aim:|Steady:|Gate:|View:" <modlog>`.

What each outcome means:

- *`Aim:` with the gate changing many times, "shown" one value, "largest change of magnification" under about
  0.05 %, the depth's largest step under 0.005 m, and the view steady by eye*: it was A and B, and it is out.
- *the gate changing 0 times in every `Aim:` second while the view still shakes*: the shake is not the gate. Read
  "the game's value" against "shown" (a game value that moves with the gate clear is the game's own lens; one that
  is steady with "shown" moving is the lock) and `eye_step` on `View:`; it is then neither A nor B and needs a new
  look.
- *"the evaluator's lens in 0"*: the notes are not reaching the field-of-view helper (the hold is off or passed: read
  the `Steady:` line's reasons). The fix cannot act; say which.
- *"the game's value over the lens"* (review 24): one value (xA to xB the same to the third decimal) in a second with
  the gate changing and the lens moving says the hooked call's value is built from this frame's lens. A spread there
  that is not in the seconds with the gate clear says it lags the lens by a frame; the hold is made for that too
  (a quiet frame's factor), so then compare "shown" (one value or not) and say so. A spread with the gate clear too
  says the value is not the lens scaled in the tangent at all.
- *near a ledge, the gate set most of the time with a lone clear frame*: "the optic's own zoom held in" should be 0
  or few frames, and "shown" moves with "the game's value", as on feel.51. Many held frames there with "shown"
  jumping is review 24's BLOCKING 1 back.
- *the depth's step in a second with `Steady:` passes in full aim ("the game scales the rig")*: under 0.005 m says
  the passed frames are kept; a step of 0.1 m or so says they are not.
- *"shown" stepping at a range change*: a zoom level change happened while the gate flipped (the optic's new target
  is taken from the next clear frame, and the glide covers it); if it pops, that is the case to look at.
- *a scoped aim-in that looks wrong (the gun late to the eye)*: that is the held depth in full aim (change 2);
  `depth_steady_off.txt` switches the whole hold off for comparison.
